Real Estate Investment Platform
A disciplined, privately held platform focused on long‑term real estate value creation.

Purple Stone Corporation is a founder‑led real estate investment platform based in Washington State. The firm targets residential and small‑scale commercial assets with durable fundamentals, clear improvement pathways, and resilient cash‑flow profiles. Capital is deployed with conservative leverage, structured risk management, and a long‑term orientation.
