Portfolio

Services
How 818 Corporation operates across the asset lifecycle.
  • Acquisition. Identification of undervalued or structurally promising properties in high-growth regions, using disciplined evaluation and conservative underwriting.
  • Development & renovation. Renovation programs that prioritize structural integrity, modern design, and long-term durability over short-term cosmetic fixes.
  • Property management. Stable, well-managed rental operations with transparent communication and responsible stewardship.
  • Disposition. Strategic sales when appropriate to realize value, rebalance risk, or reallocate capital.
Execution principles
  • Transparent budgeting and cost tracking.
  • Clear scopes of work and contractor alignment.
  • Safety and code compliance as non-negotiables.
  • Long-term maintenance planning for each asset.
Investment Strategy
Methodology, risk posture, and long-term horizon.

818 Corporation follows a structured investment methodology designed for stability and long-term value creation:

  • Market analysis. Macro and micro-level evaluation of Washington State growth corridors, including demographic trends, employment drivers, and infrastructure plans.
  • Property selection. Focus on assets with structural potential, location advantage, or renovation upside.
  • Value creation. Renovations designed for long-term durability, not short-term cosmetic shortcuts.
  • Risk management. Conservative leverage, disciplined budgeting, and operational transparency.
  • Long-term horizon. A portfolio built for stability and generational value.
Core strategies
  • Buy‑Fix‑Hold
  • Buy‑Fix‑Sell
  • Rental stabilization
  • Land banking

Strategy selection is tailored to each asset’s profile, balancing risk, capital requirements, and long-term portfolio objectives.
