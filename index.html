Real Estate Investment & Development
A purple‑stone foundation for disciplined, long‑horizon real estate stewardship.

Purple Stone Corporation is a privately held real estate investment company operating across Washington State. The firm focuses on the careful acquisition, improvement, and long‑term management of residential and small‑scale commercial assets, with an emphasis on structural integrity, conservative leverage, and generational horizons.

About Purple Stone
A formal, disciplined, founder‑led investment platform.

Mission. To acquire, strengthen, and steward real estate assets with integrity, precision, and long‑term responsibility.

Philosophy. Each property is treated as a stone in a larger foundation. Acquisitions are underwritten with discipline, renovations are executed with craftsmanship, and ownership is exercised with maturity and care.

Leadership
